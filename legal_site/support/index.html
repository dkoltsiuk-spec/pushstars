Support

Need help with Pushup Stars? Email us at beastcoredev@gmail.com. Please include your device model and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within a few days.

Push-ups are not counted

  • Place the phone in front of you, 1.5–2 meters away, so your whole body is visible.
  • Use good, even lighting and avoid a bright window behind you.
  • Go all the way down and all the way up — partial reps do not count.
  • Check that camera access is allowed: iPhone Settings → Pushup Stars → Camera.

Keep your progress

Guest progress is tied to this device. To keep it when you change phones, open Settings in the App and link your account with Apple, Google or email.

Delete your account

Open the App → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and progress from our servers. You can also request deletion by email.
