Push Stars

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Push Stars (the "App") handles your information. The App is developed and operated by Daniel Coltiuc, an individual developer based in the Republic of Moldova ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at beastcoredev@gmail.com.

The short version
  • The camera is used only to count your push-ups and check your form. Camera images and video are processed on your device and are never recorded, stored or sent to us.
  • You can play as a guest without giving us your name or email.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show third-party ads.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time in Settings.

1. Camera

When you start a workout, battle or assessment, the App asks for camera access. Camera frames are analysed in real time on your device by an on-device pose-detection model to count repetitions and evaluate technique. Frames are discarded immediately after analysis. No photo, video or image of you ever leaves your device. You can revoke camera access at any time in your device settings; the workout features will not work without it.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you open the App, we create an anonymous account identifier. If you choose to sign in with Apple, Google or email, we receive the email address and account identifier from that provider (and your name if the provider shares it). With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email address.

Profile and game data. Your display name, selected character, level, experience points, trophies, league, rewards, in-game currency, match and workout results (such as repetition counts and scores) and similar progress data. Some of this data is stored only on your device; when you have an account it may also be stored on our servers so your progress and league standings work.

Online status. While the App is open it may send a periodic signal so we can show how many players are online. This signal is linked to your account identifier.

Diagnostics. If the App crashes, we may receive a crash report containing technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version and the state of the App at the time of the crash. Crash reports are used only to fix bugs.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, health records or advertising identifiers.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the App: count repetitions, save your progress, run battles, leagues and rewards.
  • To let you sign in and restore your progress on another device.
  • To keep the game fair, for example by detecting impossible results.
  • To fix crashes and improve performance.
  • To answer your support requests.

4. Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC) for sign-in, cloud data storage, server functions, web hosting and crash reporting. Data stored with Firebase may be processed on servers in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google are provided by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies. Any future in-app purchases would be processed by Apple or Google; we never receive your payment card details.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Other players

Your display name, character, level, trophies and league position may be visible to other players, for example in leagues and battles. Other players never see camera images of you.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the App under Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the progress stored on our servers. You can also email us to request deletion. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the App. Crash reports are kept for a limited period by our provider and then deleted automatically.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact us at beastcoredev@gmail.com. We process your data to perform our agreement with you (providing the App) and on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the App secure and working.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the App.
