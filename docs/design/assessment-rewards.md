# Assessment reward

A positive 60-second assessment goes directly to the existing character reward summary. The headline reads **ASSESSMENT COMPLETE!** and the encouragement reads **Great result. Strong start!**. Actual reps, technique and earned XP populate the screen; no fitness tier is displayed. A zero-rep assessment retains the retry/skip recovery screen.

The first successful assessment credits exactly **200 Aura** and grants a welcome case, independently of the daily workout quota, in one save. The case receipt `assessment:welcome:v1` and the wallet receipt `assessment:welcome:aura:v1` survive restarts and retakes. The welcome case is an ordinary level-1 (common) case: three taps can upgrade it up to legendary, and it opens to crystals and avatar cards like any other case. Ledger v5 pays out the Aura that v4 sealed inside a fixed legendary case once, and turns that case into a fresh common one; v1–v4 inventories, wallets and receipts are preserved.

## Aura screen

The summary never counts Aura. Whenever the fight's summary carries credited Aura (today only the first assessment), its action reads **CONTINUE** and opens the standalone **AuraReward** scene, with or without a case. That screen only presents; it grants nothing. After collecting, the flow continues to this result's case, otherwise home, where the Aura flies into the home Aura counter.

The screen is a "+N AURA" meme stamp on black: the amount slams in with a flash, shake, RGB split, shockwave and sparks. Then the skull glitches in: the screen flickers to black and white, bars jump and the skull tears in horizontal strips, until an 808 hit locks it. The reveal cannot be skipped; **TAP TO COLLECT** then sends the skull flying into the camera and eases everything else out behind it. While waiting, a short glitch relapses every few seconds.

`AuraStampPresentation` animates the rig and `AuraStampFxGraphic` draws the procedural effects. Both are deterministic: they never consume gameplay randomness, never intercept taps, and reset on interruption. Art and sound are generated by scripts: `tools/art/paint_aura_skull.py` and `tools/audio/make_aura_stamp.py` / `make_aura_skull.py` / `make_reward_whooshes.py` (collect and screen-seam whooms). Their hit timings must match `AuraStampPresentation.Impact`, `SkullStart` and `SkullLock`.

## Seams between screens

Reward screens never hard-cut. `ScreenTransition` (a persistent overlay) covers the old screen, activates the next scene under the cover, and waits until a few frames run smoothly. Only then does it open an iris from the centre, where the next hero element sits. Scenes are streamed in ahead of time (`FightScreenNavigation.Preload`) only where a loading hitch is invisible: the Aura screen preloads its exit on its black first frames. Entrances (case pop-in, gem pop-in, stamp) likewise wait for `ScreenTransition.Settle()`, so a load stall lands on a still frame, never mid-animation.

| Seam | Cover | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| Summary → Aura | 0.22 s to black, instant reveal (the stamp slams out of the dark) | stamp riser |
| Aura → case / home | closes 0.55–0.8 s after the tap, as the skull finishes flying into the camera | `aura_whoosh` (sub drop tails over the load) |
| Summary / prize → case / home | 0.3 s, iris reveal | `ui_transition` (pass = cover end) |
| Home arrival | icons burst out of the opening iris | `reward_burst`, rising `reward_tick`s, `reward_complete` |

HUD counters are held at their old values (`HudBalanceHold`) until the flying icons land, then count up with them. The music bed never restarts: it nearly drops out on the Aura screen, sits back on case screens, and swells back in at home (ducking is fast, recovery slow, and never jumps on a long load frame).

## Authoring and validation

Author scenes: **Tools → Push Stars → Rewards → Configure Assessment Aura**. It regenerates `AuraReward.unity` from CaseReward's canvas and adds it to Build Settings. It also removes the Aura rigs from RewardSummary, CaseOpening and CaseReward. Dirty target scenes are rejected.

Validation menus:
- **Validate Assessment Aura** runs the 16-case economy regression, checks the scene bindings, and renders every screen at 390×844, 320×568 and 430×932.
- **Validate Assessment Flow In Play Mode** walks the real screen actions with a wallet-free preview: summary, Aura screen (early and repeated taps), three upgrade taps, opening, then the gem prize.
- **Render Aura Stamp Movie** exports 60 fps frames of the full stamp sequence to `output/aura-stamp/movie`.

Reports and images are in `output/assessment-rewards`; the economy report is `Logs/case-rewards-regression.txt`.
